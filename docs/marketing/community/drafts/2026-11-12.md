## 1. HN — Show HN: Yogurt (open-source Granola, lokale transcriptie)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50454625 (±21 u, ±95 reacties, voorpagina)

**Waarom:** transcriptie-draad. `walthamstow` vraagt hoe je sprekers herkent; de maker leest namen uit Zoom/Meet, wat niet werkt voor opnames zonder app. Daar heeft sinbadbuilds echte ervaring (AssemblyAI-diarisatie voor interviews/colleges). Plaats als **antwoord op walthamstow** ("I have what's been said, but not who said it").

> Reading names out of the meeting app is a neat trick for calls. The harder case is a recording with no app around it: an in-person interview, a lecture, a podcast file. There you need diarization from the audio itself, and whisper alone doesn't do that; the usual local route is to add pyannote on top. For my own project I went with a hosted API (AssemblyAI) for that step, which is exactly the trade-off Yogurt is trying to avoid, so it depends on what matters more to you. Either way, telling the diarizer how many speakers to expect makes a big difference when you know it.

## 2. HN — Humble Bundle just killed most of their security through vibecoding
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50463899 (±6 u, ±180 reacties)

**Waarom:** vibe-coding-kwaliteit, maar nu over veiligheid (2FA weg, login-codes uur vertraagd). Andere invalshoek dan de concepten van 11-11 (daar: CLAUDE.md/LESSONS.md): hier risicoklassen, fail-closed webhook, e2e-tests op login.

> Solo dev here, and most of my code is written by Claude Code. What I learned is that "review everything" doesn't hold up, so I sort the code by how badly it can break. Login, payments and anything that touches credits have their own rule in the agent's instructions: no change ships without a test that reproduces the old behaviour, a check in production after the deploy, and when it's unsure it has to stop and ask me instead of deciding. The payment webhook fails closed, so without a valid signature it does nothing. And the end-to-end suite logs in with real test accounts, so a login flow that suddenly behaves differently fails before anyone notices it in production. None of this is AI-specific, it's what a careful team did anyway; the tool just makes skipping it feel free.

### 5. What do you do with research notes once a project is finished?
https://forum.obsidian.md/t/what-do-you-do-with-research-notes-once-a-project-is-finished/120011 (30-10, 3 reacties)
**Waarom:** bron-notities vs onderwerp-notities; precies hoe sinbadbuilds colleges in Obsidian gebruikt.

> I keep one note per source (a lecture or interview: the transcript plus a few lines of my own) and topic notes that link into them. The topic notes are what I reread. The source notes I only open to check a quote. When a project ends I don't move anything, the topic notes just stop growing.
