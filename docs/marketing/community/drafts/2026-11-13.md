## 1. HN — Ask HN: How do you market your consumer product
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50470809 (±17 u, 9 reacties)

**Waarom:** B2B-verkoper vraagt hoe je een consumentenproduct op gang krijgt. Draad is dun en niemand geeft echte cijfers; sinbadbuilds heeft die (Ads aug–sep: ±€460, 22 signups, 0 aankopen). Top-level reactie.

> Real numbers from my side: about €460 on Google Ads got me 22 signups and zero purchases. The ads brought people who wanted one thing done once, the free tier covered it, and nobody came back a second day. So before paying for traffic I'd check if anyone returns on their own. If not, ads just buy you more one-time visitors.
